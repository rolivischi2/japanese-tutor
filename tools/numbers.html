Tens (multiples of 10)

Three composition rules
  • · 11–19: じゅう + ones  →  11 = じゅういち
  • · 20, 30, … 90: ones + じゅう  →  20 = にじゅう
  • · 21–99: tens + ones  →  25 = にじゅう + ご = にじゅうご

Reading variants: 4 = よん (also し), 7 = なな (also しち), 9 = きゅう (also く). The builder uses the most common forms; the alternatives appear in some compound counters and idioms.
