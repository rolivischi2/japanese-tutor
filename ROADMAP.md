# Roadmap

## Six-Month Plan (~7 hours/week, ~60 min/day with one rest day)

| Month | Module(s) | Primary outcomes | Listening targets | Anki state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | M00 → M01 | Fluent kana sight-reading; self-introduction 60 s | Comprehensible Japanese Complete Beginner, 4× 5-min/wk | Hiragana deck retired; Kaishi 1.5k at 300 |
| 2 | M02 → early M03 | Existence + location + start adjectives; describe a room | + Nihongo con Teppei Beginners, 5× 4-min/wk | ~600 Kaishi |
| 3 | finish M03 → M04 | Verbs polite; first 30 kanji; daily schedule | + Game Gengo 1×/wk | ~900 Kaishi + 30 kanji |
| 4 | M05 | Te-form mastery; instructions, ongoing actions | + Japanese with Shun 2×/wk | ~1100 + 55 kanji + 30 mined |
| 5 | M06 | Plain forms; narrate yesterday; understand casual native input | + YUYU 1×/wk; first casual-form CIJ dialogues | ~1300 + 85 kanji + 80 mined |
| 6 | M07 | Casual speech + particles; first 5-min natural conversation; switch tutor sessions mostly to plain form | Kaname Naito 1×/wk; first drama — start *Nagi no Oitoma* slowly | Kaishi finished + ~120 kanji + ~150 mined |

## Six-Month Checkpoint Goals

By the end of month 6, the learner should be able to:

1. Hold a 5-min conversation in plain form on familiar topics with a forgiving native speaker.
2. Understand ~60% of *Nihongo con Teppei* main podcast.
3. Read pure-kana children's content fluently.
4. Recognise ~120 kanji in vocab context.
5. Have an active vocab of ~1500–1700 words.

Approximately strong JLPT N5 / weak N4 on paper, with notably stronger spoken/listening skills than a typical N5 passer.

## Twelve-Month Checkpoint Goals

By the end of month 12, the learner should be able to:

1. Hold a 15-min unprepared conversation on varied topics.
2. Understand ~70% of native-adult podcasts.
3. Follow a slice-of-life drama at 0.85× speed with Japanese subs.
4. Recognise ~600+ kanji in context.
5. Produce both polite and plain registers fluidly.
6. Survive a 2-week independent trip to Japan.

Roughly JLPT N3-grammar / weak-N3-reading / strong-N4-listening — but with notably better spoken Japanese than a typical paper-N3 holder.

## Plan Adjustment Triggers

These conditions warrant modifying the plan:

1. **Anki mature-card retention drops below 85% for 2 weeks** → halve new cards/day; don't advance modules.
2. **Tutor reports over-translation from English** → push input ratio +10% and skip output sessions for a week.
3. **Pitch-accent recordings still sound flat at end of Module 03** → escalate Dogen from M04 to M02.
4. **Learner reaches end of M07 ahead of schedule** → run M08–M10 in parallel (they don't strongly depend on each other).
5. **Japan trip booked** → prioritise Module 11 (keigo recognition) regardless of current module.
