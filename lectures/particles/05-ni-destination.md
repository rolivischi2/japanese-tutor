# に — destination, time, indirect object, existence

The particle **に** is one of the hardest-working particles in Japanese.
It has several distinct uses, but they all share a flavour of
**"pointing at a target"** — a place you're heading toward, a moment
in time you're anchoring to, or a person you're directing something at.

This lecture takes the four most common uses one at a time.

## (1) Destination — "to"

Use に with motion verbs like いきます ("go"), きます ("come"),
かえります ("return").

> がっこう に いきます。
> *I go to school.*

> にほん に きました。
> *I came to Japan.*

> いえ に かえります。
> *I'm going home.* (lit. "returning to the house")

## (2) Time — "at"

Use に for a specific clock time, day, or date. **Don't** use に with
relative time words like きょう ("today") or あした ("tomorrow") —
those go bare.

> しちじ に おきます。
> *I get up at 7 o'clock.*

> げつようび に いきます。
> *I'll go on Monday.*

> よる に たべます。
> *I eat in the evening.*

But:

> きょう、いきます。
> *I'll go today.* (no に)

## (3) Indirect object — "to (a person)"

When you give, send, write, or teach **something** **to someone**, that
someone takes に.

> ともだち に てがみ を かきます。
> *I write a letter to a friend.*

> はは に プレゼント を あげます。
> *I give a present to my mother.*

> せんせい に しつもん を します。
> *I ask the teacher a question.* (lit. "do a question to the teacher")

## (4) Existence location — "in / at"

With the existence verbs **います** (animate) and **あります**
(inanimate), the place where something exists takes に. This is the use
that gets confused with で — see the next lecture.

> いえ に います。
> *I'm at home.*

> つくえ の うえ に ほん が あります。
> *There's a book on the desk.*

> こうえん に こども が います。
> *There are children in the park.*

## Quick summary

| Use | Example | Gloss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Destination | がっこう に いきます | go **to** school |
| Time | しちじ に おきます | get up **at** 7 |
| Indirect object | ともだち に かきます | write **to** a friend |
| Existence | いえ に います | be **at** home |
