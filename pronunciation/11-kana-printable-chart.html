Don't mix these up — the difference is the whole word

き vs さ — き has the extra cross stroke; さ does not.
ぬ vs め — ぬ ends in a loop; め has none.
る vs ろ — る closes into a loop; ろ stops short.
は vs ほ — ほ has an extra top line.
ね · れ · わ — loop = ね · straight hook = れ · open hook = わ.
し vs つ — し curves down a vertical; つ curves across the top.

The two rules

゛ dakuten (two ticks) "switches the voice on": k→g · s→z · t→d · h→b.
゜ handakuten (small circle) only ever turns h→p.
A small ゃ ゅ ょ blends with an "i"-kana into one beat (きゃ = kya, not ki-ya).

Other katakana look-alikes

ウ · ク · ワ — ウ has a top tick · ク has a sharp shoulder · ワ is wide & open.
ロ · ヨ · ユ — ロ is a closed box · ヨ has 3 prongs · ユ opens at the top.
ア · マ · ヤ — check the inner stroke: ア hooks · マ is plain · ヤ slants.
テ vs チ — テ is flat & level; チ tilts like ち.
ナ vs メ — ナ keeps a right angle; メ fully crosses.
ノ · ソ · ン — ノ is one slash; add a tick → ソ (down) or ン (up).
