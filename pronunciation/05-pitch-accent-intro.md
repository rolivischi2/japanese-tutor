# Pitch Accent Introduction

## Why Pitch Accent Matters More Than Textbooks Admit

TODO: Expand the three reasons from INSTRUCTIONS.md §3.4:
1. **Understandability gap**: pitch errors significantly impair comprehension in phone calls, noisy environments, and when the listener is not expecting a foreigner.
2. **Lexical minimal pairs**: cite the classic 橋 (hashi, bridge) LH vs 箸 (hashi, chopsticks) HL vs 端 (hashi, edge) LH+drop-on-particle — three different lexical items, identical segmentally.
3. **Re-learning cost**: pitch accent is much harder to correct after it has been produced incorrectly for years. The Wikipedia pitch-accent article states "incorrect pitch accent is a characteristic of a 'foreign accent' in Japanese."

## What Pitch Accent Is

TODO: Define pitch accent: Japanese uses two pitch levels (high H, low L) per mora. The pitch drops at most once per word (the "drop position"). There is no stress in the English sense — all morae receive equal duration and volume; only the pitch changes.
