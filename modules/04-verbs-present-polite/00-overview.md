## Verb Groups

TODO: Explain the three-group classification with modern and classical terminology:
- Group 1 / Godan / u-verbs: ends in consonant + u sound (any of: う、つ、る、く、ぐ、す、ぬ、ぶ、む)
- Group 2 / Ichidan / ru-verbs: final syllable is い-row or え-row before る (e.g. たべる、みる)
- Identification rule: if a verb ends in る AND the mora before る is in the い/え column, it is likely Group 2; otherwise Group 1. Exceptions exist.
- Group 3 / Irregular: only する and くる

## Key Particles

TODO: Cover new particles introduced in this module:
