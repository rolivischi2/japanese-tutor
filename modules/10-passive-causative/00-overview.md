## Adversative / Suffering Passive

TODO: Explain this uniquely Japanese construction in detail — it has no English equivalent:
- Subject is negatively affected by an action that happens around them
- The acting entity is marked with に
- Examples: 雨にふられた ("was rained on" — the rain fell on me, adversely); 子供になかれた ("the child cried on me" — affected by the crying)
- This construction is extremely common in complaint/narrative speech and will appear frequently in native input after M06.
