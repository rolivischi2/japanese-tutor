## Sentence-Ending Particles

TODO: Cover each particle with core function, intonation, and 3 example sentence stubs:
- **ね**: seeking shared feeling/agreement; falling tone. Cannot be used with strongly new information.
- **よ**: giving new information to listener; soft falling tone. Avoid overdoing — sounds aggressive if overused.
- **よね**: information + check with listener (よ + ね combination; NOT ねよ which sounds wrong).
- **な / なあ**: masculine; reflective; talking to self or seeking mild agreement; attaches to plain form.
- **かな**: "I wonder if…"; self-directed musing.
- **か**: in casual speech often dropped; rising intonation alone marks a question.
- **の**: questioning or explanatory ending in casual (feminine-neutral).
- **んだ / なんだ**: explanatory/emphasis ending. Links to なんですか / んですか in polite.
