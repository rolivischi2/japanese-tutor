# Module 01 — Self-Talk Prompts

Self-talk is how Module 01 grammar moves from "I can write it" to "I can say it
without thinking." Follow the protocol in `pedagogy/05-self-talk-protocol.md`:
pick a prompt, speak it aloud, **record yourself in Voice Memo**, listen back,
then re-record until it flows.

All output here is **polite form** (です / じゃありません) — Module 01 stays
polite so you can use it directly in tutor sessions.

**Pronunciation watch-list while recording** (see `pronunciation/`):
- は as a particle = "wa," never "ha."
- です = "dess" — devoiced final す, lips **not** rounded on う.
- Every mora equally loud — do not thump the first mora.
- か rises at the end of a question.

---

## Tier 1 — Single sentences (warm-up)

Say each as one clean sentence. Repeat until there is no pause before です.

1. State your name. → わたし は ____ です。
2. State your nationality. → わたし は ____じん です。
3. State your age. → わたし は ____ さい です。 *(20 → はたち)*
4. State your occupation. → わたし は ____ です。
5. Say one thing you are **not**. → わたし は ____ じゃありません。
6. Point at something near you and name it. → これ は ____ です。
7. Point at something far away and name it. → あれ は ____ です。
8. Name something that belongs to you. → これ は わたし の ____ です。

---

## Tier 2 — Mini self-introduction (3–4 sentences)

String Tier 1 sentences together. Record the whole run as one take.

9. Name + nationality + occupation.
10. Name + age + occupation + one negative.
11. Introduce a friend: their name, their nationality, "they are my friend."
    → ともだち の ____ です。____さん は ____じん です。
12. Describe two objects on your desk (これ / それ / あれ) and say whose one is.

---

## Tier 3 — Ask-and-answer (play both roles)

Speak the question, pause, then answer it out loud as yourself. This rehearses
the back-and-forth of a real conversation.

13. "Are you a student?" → answer truthfully.
14. "Are you Japanese?" → answer truthfully, then add your real nationality.
15. "Is this your phone?" → answer, using わたし の.
16. "Which one is your bag?" → どれ が…？ → answer with これ / それ / あれ.
17. "What is that over there?" → あれ は なん です か。 → answer.

---

## Tier 4 — The milestone: 60-second unprepared self-introduction

This is the **Module 01 milestone** (`progress/milestones.md`, M2): *introduce
yourself, unprepared, for 60 seconds.*

18. Set a 60-second timer. Introduce yourself with no script and no pauses to
    plan. Cover, in any order:
    - はじめまして and your name
    - your nationality (and that you are **not** Japanese)
    - your age
    - your occupation (and one occupation you are **not**)
    - one possession sentence (これ は わたし の ____ です)
    - close with どうぞ よろしく

Record it. Listen back and check three things:
- Did every sentence end correctly in です / じゃありません / です か?
- Did any です land on a verb? (It must not — ✗いきますです.)
- Was は always "wa"? Was the first mora of each word kept light?

Re-record until you can fill the full 60 seconds with no English, no long
silences, and no grammar slips. When you can, the milestone is met.

---

## Suggested rotation

One 5-minute self-talk session per day:
- Days 1–2: Tier 1.
- Days 3–4: Tier 2.
- Days 5–6: Tier 3.
- Day 7 and onward: Tier 4 — re-record the 60-second introduction daily until
  it is automatic. Bring your best take to your next tutor session.
