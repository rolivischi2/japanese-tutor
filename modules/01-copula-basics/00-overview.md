# Module 01 — Copula Basics

This is your first grammar module. By the end of it you can build, negate, and
question simple noun sentences in polite Japanese — the backbone of every
self-introduction. No kanji yet: everything is in kana.

## What this module teaches

You will be able to say things like *"I am an engineer," "This is not coffee,"
"Is that your phone?"* and *"My book."* That is enough to introduce yourself
and talk about people, objects, and possession.

## Grammar points

In order, one file per point:

1. **`01-desu-copula.md`** — です as the polite copula. です states *identity*
   (X = Y), it is not the verb "to be / to exist." It never inflects for
   person, number, or gender. Word order is copula-final. Plain だ is noted but
   deferred to Module 06.
2. **`02-wa-topic-particle.md`** — は, the topic particle, read **"wa."** は
   presents a topic ("as for X…"), it does not mark the grammatical subject.
   Cure Dolly framing: は flags a topic; the underlying が-subject may be elided.
3. **`03-negative-dewa-arimasen.md`** — ではありません and the full register
   spectrum: ではありません > じゃありません > じゃないです > じゃない / ではない.
4. **`04-questions-ka.md`** — か at the end of a sentence makes a question. No
   word-order inversion. Answering with はい / いいえ.
5. **`05-no-possession.md`** — の for possession and noun-modification
   (わたしのほん = "my book").
6. **`06-kosoado-preview.md`** — これ / それ / あれ / どれ, a partial preview of
   the こそあど system (the full set arrives in Module 02).

Then practice material:

- **`dialogues.md`** — three short dialogues, all kana, each line glossed.
- **`exercises.md`** — drills with an answer key at the bottom.
- **`self-talk.md`** — speaking prompts to record yourself doing.

## The one mistake to never make

✗ **いきますです** — です does **not** attach to verbs. です links nouns. Verbs
(いきます etc.) carry their own politeness and arrive in Module 04. If you ever
catch yourself sticking です onto a verb, stop. This module forbids it.

## Milestone (from `progress/milestones.md`, M2)

**Introduce yourself unprepared for 60 seconds.** Name, nationality, age,
occupation, a couple of negatives ("I am not a teacher"), and one possession
sentence — produced live, without a script. `self-talk.md` builds you up to it.

## How to work through it

1. Read `01`–`06` in order. Do the inline checks as you go.
2. Read `dialogues.md` aloud twice each.
3. Do `exercises.md`, then self-check against the answer key.
4. Record `self-talk.md` prompts in Voice Memo. Re-record until the 60-second
   self-introduction flows without pausing to think.
