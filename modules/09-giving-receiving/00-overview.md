## Honorific Tier

TODO: Cover the three formal/humble counterparts:
- さしあげる: formal equivalent of あげる — rarely used in modern Japanese; flag social risk of sounding overly deferential or archaic.
- くださる: formal/respectful equivalent of くれる — ubiquitous. ください is its imperative.
- いただく: humble equivalent of もらう — most useful for a foreigner; very widely expected in formal contexts.
