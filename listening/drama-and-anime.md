## Anime Notes

TODO: Explain the anime caveat clearly: anime is NOT banned — it is deprioritised as primary input before M07 because:
1. Many anime use heavily stylised speech (shouting, formal-with-friend register, archaic grammar)
2. Masculine-coded sentence-final particles (ぞ、ぜ、だぜ) are overrepresented and distort output register
3. Fantasy/action vocabulary is low-frequency in real conversation
Recommended anime for later stages: slice-of-life genres (日常系) such as よつばと! (manga), Shirokuma Cafe, Barakamon.
