# Shopping

What you actually need at the konbini, supermarket, or boutique: price,
size, availability, payment.

## Price

> これ は いくら ですか。
> *How much is this?*

> たかい です。
> *It's expensive.* (you can mutter this to yourself; not actually rude
> to say out loud about a price)

> やすい です。
> *It's cheap.*

## Size and fit

> ちいさい です。
> *It's small.*

> おおきい です。
> *It's big.*

> ちょうど いい です。
> *It's just right.*

## Availability

> これ、ありますか。
> *Do you have this?* (hold up the item or point — ありますか
> literally means "does it exist?")

> ありません。
> *We don't have any.* (the negative — the staff's likely reply if it's
> out of stock)

> あります。
> *We have it.* / *Yes, there is.*

## Payment

> クレジットカード、つかえますか。
> *Can I use a credit card?* (still worth asking in older shops;
> contactless is now common but cash remains king in many small places)

> げんきん で おねがいします。
> *In cash, please.*

> ふくろ、いりません。
> *I don't need a bag.* (since 2020 most shops charge for plastic bags
> and ask; this is the polite refusal)

## Closing

> ありがとう ございます。
> *Thank you very much.*

## Notes on register

- A bare **ありがとう** is fine with peers; in a shop or with the
  staff, always add **ございます**.
- Pointing + これ ください + a tiny bow can carry you through almost
  any shopping interaction even if you forget every other word.
