# Greetings

The basic daily greetings, organised roughly by time of day, then a few
common arrival / departure formulas.

## Time-of-day greetings

> おはよう ございます。
> *Good morning.* (polite — drop ございます for casual peers: おはよう)

> こんにちは。
> *Hello. / Good day.* (use during daytime, roughly 10am–5pm)

> こんばんは。
> *Good evening.*

> おやすみなさい。
> *Good night.* (said before going to bed; casual: おやすみ)

## Hellos and goodbyes

> さようなら。
> *Goodbye.* (fairly formal / long-term; for "see you later" with peers,
> use じゃ、また or またね)

> またね。
> *See you (later).* (casual)

> じゃ、また あした。
> *Well then, see you tomorrow.*

## Coming and going (home, office)

> ただいま。
> *I'm home.* / *I'm back.* (said when entering your own home or
> returning to your desk)

> おかえりなさい。
> *Welcome back.* (the reply to ただいま; casual: おかえり)

> いってきます。
> *I'm off.* (said when leaving the house; literally "I'll go and come back")

> いってらっしゃい。
> *Take care, see you.* (the reply to いってきます)

## Shops and service

> いらっしゃいませ。
> *Welcome (to our shop).* (you'll hear this every time you walk into a
> store or restaurant — you are not expected to reply)

## Notes on register

- **ございます** at the end of おはよう makes it polite. Among close
  friends and family, just おはよう is normal.
- こんにちは and こんばんは don't have casual versions — they're the
  default in any social setting.
- The ending kana on こんにちは and こんばんは is **は**, pronounced
  **"wa"** (historical particle spelling).
