## The Five-Step Protocol

TODO: Write out each step with timing guidance:
1. Listen once, eyes closed — absorb prosody and rhythm without analysing
2. Listen again with transcript — map audio to text
3. Shadow with transcript at ~0.5-second delay — synchronise mouth to speaker
4. Shadow without transcript — rely on internalised audio
5. Record own voice and compare — identify pitch accent and timing errors
